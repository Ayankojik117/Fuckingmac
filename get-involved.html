Get involved

Why you should join MaC Foundation

Volunteering here isn't just giving your time to good causes — it's building real, transferable skills while you do it.

Core benefits

What you'll walk away with

Leadership Skills

Lead a project from idea to delivery, and lead a team while doing it.

Communication Skills

Pitch ideas, coordinate teams, and speak to the community you're serving.

Social Media Management

Run real campaigns across Instagram, YouTube, and more for a live audience.

Photography / Videography

Document projects and tell their story visually, from field to feed.

Computer & AI Tech

Use modern tools to plan, design, and scale what we do.

Content Creation

Write, design, and produce content that moves people to act.

Teamwork & Collaboration

Work alongside volunteers from different backgrounds toward one goal.

Problem-Solving & Critical Thinking

Every project has a snag — you'll learn to think on your feet.

Empathy & Compassion

Work directly with the people your project is meant to serve.

Civic Responsibility

Build a genuine sense of purpose and ownership over your community.

Project Design & Management

Scope, plan, and run a project end-to-end — real management experience.

Networking & Credibility

Build relationships and a track record that carries beyond MaC.

Advocacy & Policy Engagement

Learn to speak up for the causes you work on — and understand how change happens at a systemic level, not just a project level.
