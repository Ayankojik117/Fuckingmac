NGO, community, founded on small steps

Change doesn't wait for the perfect moment. It starts with a step.

MaC Foundation is a youth-driven community working across Social Welfare, Sports & Mental Wellness, Environmental Sustainability, Women Empowerment, and Education — one project, one volunteer, one step at a time.

What we believe

Meaningful change begins with small steps.

Empowering Change Through Every Step.

We're not waiting for permission to build a better community. MaC Foundation brings young people together to design, run, and lead real projects — and grow into leaders while doing it.

MaC Foundation started with a simple idea: you don't need permission to start making a change, you just need to take the first step.

Our five pillars

Where we work

01

Community outreach and support for those who need it most.

Direct community support — outreach drives, donation collection, and support for underserved groups in our neighborhoods. This is the pillar that puts volunteers face-to-face with the people they're helping.

Activities

  • Outreach drives
  • Donation collection
  • Support for underserved groups in our neighborhoods
Read more about Social Welfare

02

Movement and mindset — building resilience on and off the field.

Sports days, fitness sessions, and open conversations about mental health. We treat physical movement and mental wellness as one pillar because, for the young people we work with, they usually are.

Activities

  • Sports days
  • Fitness sessions
  • Open conversations about mental health
Read more about Sports & Mental Wellness

03

Clean-ups, plantation drives, and everyday climate action.

Clean-up drives, tree plantation, and awareness campaigns that make sustainability a habit, not a one-off event, in the communities we work in.

Activities

  • Clean-up drives
  • Tree plantation
  • Awareness campaigns
Read more about Environmental Sustainability

04

Skill-building and advocacy that expand what's possible.

Skill-building workshops, mentorship, and advocacy that open doors — built and led largely by the young women in our own volunteer base.

Activities

  • Skill-building workshops
  • Mentorship
  • Advocacy
Read more about Women Empowerment

05

Access, mentorship, and learning support for every step forward.

Learning support, mentorship, and access initiatives for students who need an extra hand — because education is the pillar that carries into every other one.

Activities

  • Learning support
  • Mentorship
  • Access initiatives
Read more about Education

Why join MaC

Skills you build while you give back

Every project you run at MaC leaves you with more than a memory — it leaves you with a skill.

Everything you'll walk away with

  • Lead a project from idea to delivery, and lead a team while doing it.

  • Pitch ideas, coordinate teams, and speak to the community you're serving.

  • Run real campaigns across Instagram, YouTube, and more for a live audience.

  • Document projects and tell their story visually, from field to feed.

  • Use modern tools to plan, design, and scale what we do.

  • Write, design, and produce content that moves people to act.

  • Work alongside volunteers from different backgrounds toward one goal.

  • Every project has a snag — you'll learn to think on your feet.

  • Work directly with the people your project is meant to serve.

  • Build a genuine sense of purpose and ownership over your community.

  • Scope, plan, and run a project end-to-end — real management experience.

  • Build relationships and a track record that carries beyond MaC.

  • Learn to speak up for the causes you work on — and understand how change happens at a systemic level, not just a project level.
